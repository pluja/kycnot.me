Account trust on [KYCnot.me](https://kycnot.me) comes from what you have done on the site, not from a points score. Published comments, proof a moderator has checked, approved service suggestions and the age of your account unlock features. Recent rejections for breaking the rules limit what you can post.

Your own numbers and unlocks are on your [account page](/account).

## Creating an account

You can [create an account](/account/generate) to suggest new services or share your feedback on service pages.

User accounts do not require any personal information. Your username will be **randomly** generated to prevent impersonation and protect your privacy.

When you create an account, you are given a **login key**. Login keys are **displayed only once**. Be sure to **store it securely**, as it **cannot be recovered** if lost. It is recommended to use a password manager like [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com) or [KeePassXC](https://keepassxc.org/).

## What counts toward account trust

- **Published comments:** experiences and discussions that are live on a service page. Replies don't count.
- **Comments with checked proof:** published comments whose attached proof a moderator checked and accepted.
- **Approved suggestions:** new services and edits you suggested that were approved. Suggestions on a service you represent don't count.
- **Account age:** days since you created the account.

## Badges

Badges next to a name tell you who is posting.

| Badge          | What it means                                                                                   |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Admin          | Runs KYCnot.me.                                                                                 |
| Moderator      | Reviews comments and checks proof for KYCnot.me.                                                |
| _Service_ team | Works for that service. We confirmed it before adding the badge.                                |
| Veteran        | An older account with a good record. It's earned, see [below](#how-do-i-get-the-veteran-badge). |
| New account    | The account was less than 30 days old when it posted.                                           |
| Single comment | The author has posted only once.                                                                |

People who help us with one task, like reviewing cases, don't get a badge.

Rejections for other reasons, such as duplicates or off-topic comments, don't count. Neither do rejections older than 90 days.

- **2 or more counting rejections in 90 days:** your new comments wait for a moderator before they go live.
- **4 or more spam rejections in 90 days:** you can't post or reply.

While either limit applies, every unlock above is paused. Both limits lift on their own once the older rejections pass 90 days. Accounts that moderators flag as spammers can't post at all.

## Verified and affiliated users

**Verified users** have proven their identity by linking their account to a specific website. This verification confirms they are legitimate representatives of that site, whether it's a personal website, blog, social media profile, or service page. You can request verification [in your profile](/account).

**Affiliated users** are users who represent a service listed in the directory, such as owners, support staff, or team members. If you represent a service and want to become affiliated, you can request it [in your profile](/account).

### Claiming a service

If you run or work for a listed service, you can claim it yourself with a DNS record:

1. Open [Claim a service](/account/claim), or "I work for this service" on the service's page.
2. Add a TXT record to the service's website domain with the value `kycnotme-verify=<your-username>`. Several people can share one record: `kycnotme-verify=name1,name2`.
3. Pick your role and press **Check and claim**. The affiliation is added as soon as the record is found.

Only the website the service lists as its own counts, not pages on shared platforms or hosts behind a CNAME. Keep the record: we check it every day and notify you when it's missing. After three misses in a row, or if the domain changes owners, the affiliation is removed.

A claimed affiliation shows on your profile and comments as **claimed**. Our team verifies it before it opens the private files of cases against the service, and lists you as the owner only on request. To ask for either, or if the service has no website of its own, [contact us](/contact?category=ACCOUNT_VERIFICATION).
