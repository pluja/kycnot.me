## Using our name and logo

You're welcome to show that your service is on KYCnot.me. For example:

- A "Listed on KYCnot.me" or "Review us on KYCnot.me" link to your page, or the badge from your service page.
- Your current KYC level or score, with a link to your page so people can check it.
- Asking your customers to leave an honest review.

Please don't:

- Call us a partner, or say we endorse or verified your service when your page doesn't say so. We don't accept partnerships.
- Show a score or rating we never gave, or an old one.
- Use our name or logo if your service isn't listed.
- Pay for reviews, reward them with discounts or bonuses, or ask only happy customers to write one.
- Pretend to be KYCnot.me or our staff.

If we see misuse, we'll first ask you to fix it. If it isn't fixed within 14 days, or it happens again, we add a public note to your listing, or decline your suggestion.

Please, link back to [KYCnot.me](https://kycnot.me) when possible, and use responsibly.

You can link to a service's review section with: `https://kycnot.me/service/[slug]/review`
